# REQUiZA design notes
Palette (cream, black, fuchsia, teal): cream #F4EBDD, ink #121212, fuchsia #D81B8C (promises, danger), teal #0E7C7B (realistic growth, safe), mute #5A544B. Violet #7A5AF8 only in the quantum-inspired lab.
Type: American Captain for display, nav, buttons and numerals; Inter for body text.
Layout: golden-ratio column splits (1.618fr : 1fr); Z-pattern on landing; dark hero on sign-in and sign-up.
Motion: 650-750 ms eased page curtain, 750 ms content fade-up; all disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
Rules: no guaranteed-return language, no advice, no certification of any app or company, no collection of credentials.
